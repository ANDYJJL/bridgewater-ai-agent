# Human Review and Control Plan

This plan describes where human analysts remain in control of the
Bridgewater-style AI investment research workflow. The AI coworker supports
research, drafting, scenario analysis, and debate. It does not approve, route,
or execute trades.

| Process step | What the AI does | What the human does | Human approval required? | What could go wrong? | What should happen if there is a problem? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Research assignment intake | Parses the selected ticker, company, theme, and question; loads or generates the latest backend packet. | Junior Analyst confirms the assignment is in scope and chooses `NVDA` or `KO`. | Yes, before relying on the packet. | Wrong ticker, vague question, or unsupported company. | Block unsupported tickers, show validation warning, and ask the analyst to revise the assignment. |
| 2. Source collection and packet generation | Runs deterministic tools for SEC, news, macro, market, indicator, valuation, entry price, and stress-test artifacts. | Junior Analyst checks data availability and missing-data warnings. | Yes, if the evidence is missing or stale. | Data may be unavailable, stale, or incomplete. | Surface limitations in the packet and UI; avoid strong action labels when key evidence is missing. |
| 3. Evidence and indicator review | Explains indicators and displays source references, RAG policy excerpts, filing evidence, news evidence, and model context. | Junior Analyst inspects whether the evidence supports the AI explanation. | Yes, before moving to synthesis. | The AI may overstate a keyword signal or retrieve a weak source. | Keep explanations grounded in source paths, show weak-evidence limitations, and allow the analyst to reject or ignore the draft. |
| 4. Junior synthesis memo draft | Drafts editable memo sections with source maps and human review notes. | Junior Analyst edits the memo, checks valuation limitations, and marks it ready for Senior Analyst review. | Yes. | Draft may sound polished while missing a caveat such as simulated share count or proxy discount rate. | Require editable sections, show evidence traceability, and require manual mark-ready action. |
| 5. Senior valuation and proposal review | Summarizes valuation basis, entry reference, experimental model checks, and proposal rationale. | Senior Analyst challenges assumptions, adjusts stance/sizing locally, adds controls, and prepares risk handoff. | Yes. | Experimental model outputs may be mistaken for final decisions or precise action targets. | Label experimental checks as review-only, keep backend proposal fields read-only, and require Senior-authored controls. |
| 6. Risk Team scenario analysis | Generates bear/base/bull scenario analysis from Senior proposal, DCF basis, risk factors, and scenario settings. | Risk Team reviews selected stress assumptions, valuation impact, portfolio impact, and control recommendations. | Yes. | Scenario may drift from selected case or repeat generic analysis. | Tests enforce selected-case discipline; UI keeps Risk Team judgment and controls editable before PM handoff. |
| 7. Portfolio Manager debate and decision review | Runs a devil's advocate debate with growth, risk, valuation, portfolio construction, and governance challengers. | Portfolio Manager answers unresolved questions and records a local decision. | Yes. | PM may skip objections or treat AI debate as approval. | Show unresolved questions and final checklist; decision remains local-only and does not execute any trade or route an external order. |
| 8. Final record and fallback handling | Stores local UI state and returns deterministic fallback text if LLM support fails. | Human reviewer checks that fallbacks, warnings, and saved local decisions are understood. | Yes, before presentation or reuse. | LLM/API failure, stale localStorage state, or mismatch between local notes and backend packet. | Display fallback status, source packet context, and limitations; rerun or reset from backend packet when needed. |
