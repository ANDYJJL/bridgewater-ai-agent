# `presentation_slides/`

Final presentation materials for June 4. Drop your deck here (PDF or
slides export). The presentation should be a **realistic walk-through**
of a user completing a real task — not a feature tour.

Required content (from the brief):

1. Current business process
2. AI-supported workflow diagram
3. Working user interface with realistic input producing useful output
4. Data and evidence used by the AI
5. A human review, edit, rejection, or approval step
6. Time, cost, or quality estimate
7. Testing AI performance — actual results from your test runs, not just predicted failures
8. What you would improve with more time

Keep the language simple. Avoid vague claims like *"AI will transform
the process."* Show specific steps that change, what the AI produces,
what the human reviews, and what assumptions you are making.
